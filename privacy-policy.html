Privacy Policy for First Mate

Last updated: July 22, 2025

1. Introduction

First Mate ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our sailing companion mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Password (encrypted and stored securely)

2.2 App Usage Data

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Checklists and checklist items you create
  • Ship's log entries (dates, times, navigation data, weather conditions, crew information)
  • Personal notes and observations
  • App preferences and settings

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide user authentication and account security
  • Enable app functionality and data synchronization
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Provide customer support when requested

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

Most of your app data (checklists, logs, notes) is stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage mechanisms.

4.2 Firebase Authentication

We use Google Firebase Authentication to manage user accounts. Firebase complies with industry-standard security practices and data protection regulations.

4.3 Security Measures

We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security updates

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With Firebase/Google for authentication services
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights or safety

6. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Firebase Authentication (Google). Please review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy for information about how Google handles your data.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account information as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or via email.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@firstmateapp.com

This privacy policy is designed to be compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and Apple's App Store requirements.
